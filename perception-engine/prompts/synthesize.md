## 1. Signal Map Summary
Key signals, tensions, catalysts extracted from the internal corpus.

## 2. Market & Sector Intelligence
Ground all external claims in the evidence cards; cite source_title inline like (Source: XYZ). Prefix each insight: [INTERNAL] internal corpus, [SECTORAL] market/industry, [TECH] technology.

## 3. Competitive & Strategic Landscape
Positioning, competitive dynamics, whitespace.

## 4. Opportunity & Risk Matrix
Top 3 opportunities and top 3 risks, each with one supporting evidence card citation.

## 5. Narrative & Messaging Intelligence
What language is working in the market. Vocabulary patterns. Proof points that resonate.

## 6. Sharpest Move
One paragraph: the single highest-leverage strategic action, grounded in the evidence. Then 3 direct implications for the brand deck.

## References
List every source cited, formatted as:
- [source_title](url) — one-line description of what it contributes

Never invent attributions. Paraphrase fully; do not reproduce source wording. Be ambitious: synthesise across sources to surface non-obvious connections.
